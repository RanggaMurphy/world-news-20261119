Trump, Xi and Putin Sit Down Together in Shenzhen — a First

The leaders of the world's three largest nuclear powers hold their first-ever three-way meeting on Thursday, on the sidelines of the APEC summit. Trump presses nuclear arms control; Ukraine is kept off the agenda.

Crimea Bridge Attacked for the Third Time in a Week

Traffic over the 19-kilometre bridge was suspended again on Thursday after a new attack, Crimea's Russian-installed governor said. Earlier strikes this week destroyed the central road section and dropped the main arch into the sea.

4MIDDLE EAST · CONFLICTSKY NEWS · AFP

Israeli Tanks Cross Gaza's Ceasefire Line

The first ground operation across the Yellow Line since last October's ceasefire: tanks rolled into central Gaza and dozens of displaced families fled their tents near Nuseirat. Israel says it is dismantling a tunnel; Hamas calls it a truce violation.

5ASIA · WEATHERIMD · THE HINDU

Cyclone Akvan Closes In on India's East Coast

The severe storm is due ashore around noon on Friday between Narsapur and Puri, winds of 90–100 km/h gusting to 110. Odisha has identified 204,070 people for possible evacuation across eight districts.

6UNITED STATES · POLITICSAP · REUTERS

Jeffries Nominated for Speaker, a Historic First

House Democrats voted 251–11 to nominate Hakeem Jeffries, putting him on track to become the first Black speaker of the House. The full chamber votes on 3 January, when the new Congress convenes.

7EUROPE · ECONOMYREUTERS · COMMISSION

EU Proposes a €45 Billion Loan for Ukraine

Brussels' loan for 2027 would cover two-thirds of Kyiv's €70 billion funding gap — €25 billion military, €20 billion budget — repaid only once Russia pays reparations.

8MARKETSREUTERS · INVESTOPEDIA

Gold Slumps to a 14-Month Low

Gold fell 3% to $3,735 an ounce, its lowest since September 2025, as rate-hike bets lifted the dollar to a 19-month high. The Dow shed 560 points and the S&P 500 fell for a fifth straight session.

9WORLD · SOCIETYPEW · AP

Islam to Become the World's Largest Religion by 2035

New Pew projections put Muslims at 2.57 billion and Christians at 2.52 billion by 2035 — a crossover driven by birth rates: 3.1 children per Muslim woman, 2.5 per Christian woman, in 2020–25.
