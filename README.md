**The Day in World News — Thursday, 19 November 2026, on one page.** A
broadsheet front page that sums up the day's world news in a single
1024×1024 image:

- **Trump, Xi and Putin sit down together in Shenzhen — a first** — the
  leaders of the world's three largest nuclear powers hold their first-ever
  three-way meeting on the sidelines of the APEC summit.
- **Crimea Bridge attacked for the third time in a week** — traffic over the
  19-kilometre bridge suspended again on Thursday; earlier strikes destroyed
  its central road section and dropped the main arch into the sea.
- **After a 13-year wait, Grand Theft Auto VI is finally here** — the game
  launches worldwide on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- **Israeli tanks cross Gaza's ceasefire line** — the first ground operation
  across the Yellow Line since last October's ceasefire.
- **Cyclone Akvan closes in on India's east coast** — 204,070 people
  identified for possible evacuation across eight districts of Odisha.
- **Jeffries nominated for Speaker, a historic first** — House Democrats vote
  251–11; he is on track to become the first Black speaker of the House.
- **EU proposes a €45 billion loan for Ukraine** — repaid only once Russia
  pays war reparations.
- **Gold slumps to a 14-month low** — down 3% to $3,735 an ounce as the
  dollar hits a 19-month high.
- **Islam to become the world's largest religion by 2035** — Pew projects
  2.57 billion Muslims against 2.52 billion Christians.

Every story was reported that day by the wires and outlets credited on the
page (Reuters, Associated Press, AFP, Sky News, BusinessWorld, the European
Commission, the India Meteorological Department, The Hindu, CNN,
Investopedia, Pew Research Center). The page is drawn in `frontpage.html`
(HTML/CSS with hand-built SVG illustrations and a dotted world map) and
rendered to `entry.png` at exactly 1024×1024.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

The program copies `entry.png` to the output directory as `entry.png` — byte
for byte, no network, the same bytes on every run.
